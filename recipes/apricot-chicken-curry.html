Apricot Chicken Curry with Baby Spinach & Basmati Rice

Cookware

  • chef's knife
  • colander
  • cutting board
  • garlic press (optional)
  • measuring cups
  • measuring spoons
  • small saucepan
  • stainless steel sauté pan
  • stirring spoon
  • tongs
  • vegetable peeler

Ingredients

  • baby spinach 1 (5oz) pkg
  • basmati rice 1 cup
  • chicken or vegetable broth 16 fl oz
  • chicken thighs, boneless skinless 2 lb
  • cilantro 1 small bunch
  • coconut milk 1 (13.5 fl oz) can
  • dried apricots 16
  • garlic 2 cloves
  • ginger root 1 (1inch) pieces
  • yellow onion 1 medium
  • black pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • curry powder
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • paprika
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Rinse rice in a strainer until water runs clear and transfer to a small saucepan. Add broth and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer, stir, and cover. Cook until liquid is absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
    • 16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    • 1 cup basmati rice

  2. Peel and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic and giner. Medium dice apricots. Transfer everything to a plate.
    • 1 medium yellow onion
    • 2 cloves garlic
    • 2 (1 inch) pieces ginger
    • 16 dried apricots

  3. Heat a sauté pan over medium heat.

  4. Trim excess fat and slice chicken into strips. Season with salt and pepper.
    • 2 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    • 1/2 tsp salt
    • 1/2 tsp black pepper

  5. Coat bottom of pan with oil. Add chicken and, stirring occassionally, cook until no longer pink, 3-4 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
    • 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

  6. Once chicken has been transferred, add more oil and onion to pan. Stirring frequently, cook until onion has softened, 3-4 minutes.
    • 1/2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

  7. Wash and dry spinach and cilantro. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves.
    • 1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    • 1 small bunch cilantro

  8. Add apricots, garlic, ginger, paprika, coriander, and curry powder to pan. Stir with onions until fragrant, 1-2 minutes.
    • 1 tsp paprika
    • 1 tsp coriander
    • 1/2 tsp curry powder

  9. Return chicken to pan. Add coconut milk, spinach, and 1/2 of the cilantro (save the rest for garnishing). Stir until well combined. Reduce heat to medium-low and cook until curry thickens, 5-10 minutes.
    • 1 (13.5 oz) can coconut milk

  10. Uncover rice and fluff with a fork.

  11. To serve, place the rice in a bowl, top with curry, and garnish with remaining cilantro. Enjoy!
